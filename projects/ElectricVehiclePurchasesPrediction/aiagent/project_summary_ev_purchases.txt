Metric      : ROC-AUC
Data        : train_cleaned.csv (668665 rows, 14 original features, id column dropped)
Date        : 20 September 2026
Goal        : top 10% leaderboard (about rank 240 of about 2400 teams)


DATASET COLUMNS
===============
Original (15 columns in train_cleaned.csv):
  id, Age, Annual_Income_USD, Daily_Commute_km, Number_of_Cars_Owned,
  Charging_Stations_Near_Home, Charging_Stations_Near_Work,
  Environmental_Concern_Level, Gender, City_Type, Current_Car_Type,
  Home_Charging_Possible, Subsidy_Available, Range_Anxiety_Level, Will_Buy_EV

Engineered (4 columns added):
  Income_Floor_Flag, Income_per_Car, Income_vs_City, Multi_Car_Flag


COLUMN DESCRIPTIONS
===================
id                           Row identifier (0-668664). Not used as a feature.
Age                          Age in years (25-69).
Annual_Income_USD            Annual income in USD (30000-188549). Clipped at 30000
                             (about 9% of rows sit exactly at the floor).
Daily_Commute_km             Daily commute distance in km (5-98.7). Clipped at 5
                             (about 22% of rows).
Number_of_Cars_Owned         Cars in the household (1-4).
Charging_Stations_Near_Home  Charging stations near home (0-14).
Charging_Stations_Near_Work  Charging stations near work (0-19).
Environmental_Concern_Level  Environmental concern, whole levels 1-5.
                             Very strong signal: buy rate rises from about 0.6%
                             at level 1 to about 52% at level 5.
Gender                       Male (55%), Female (44%), Other (1%).
City_Type                    Urban (43%), Suburban (38%), Rural (19%).
Current_Car_Type             Sedan (45%), SUV (37%), Hatchback (12%), Truck (6%).
Home_Charging_Possible       Yes (69%) / No (31%).
Subsidy_Available            Yes (63%) / No (37%). Very strong signal: buy rate is
                             about 0.6% without subsidy vs about 27% with subsidy.
Range_Anxiety_Level          Low (90%), Medium (9%), High (0.3%). High anxiety
                             almost never buys.
Will_Buy_EV                  TARGET. Yes (17.5%) / No (82.5%).

Weak signals: Age, Number_of_Cars_Owned and both charging-station counts show
little relation to the target in this data.

Engineered features (computed without the target, no leakage):
Income_Floor_Flag   1 if Annual_Income_USD <= 30000, else 0.
Income_per_Car      Annual_Income_USD / Number_of_Cars_Owned.
Income_vs_City      Annual_Income_USD / mean income of the same City_Type
                    (mean taken from TRAIN only, reused for test).
Multi_Car_Flag      1 if Number_of_Cars_Owned >= 2, else 0.


MODEL AND STEPS TAKEN
=====================
Best model: CatBoostClassifier (gradient boosting on decision trees, handles the
6 categorical columns natively) with tuned parameters and the 4 engineered
features. Libraries: Python, pandas, numpy, scikit-learn, CatBoost, Optuna.

Steps:
1. Cleaning + EDA: stripped whitespace in categorical columns, checked duplicates,
   value ranges and outliers. No rows were removed (ids 0-668664 intact) and
   there are no missing values.
2. Model screening (100k-row sample, hold-out AUC): CatBoost 0.94007,
   HistGradientBoosting 0.93995, LightGBM 0.93949, XGBoost 0.93943,
   Logistic Regression 0.93874, Random Forest 0.93628 -> CatBoost selected.
3. Hyperparameter tuning: Optuna (TPE sampler) on one stratified 80/20 hold-out,
   with early stopping. Results below.
4. Feature engineering: 14 candidate features tested one at a time on top of the
   best set so far (single hold-out); 4 kept (listed above).
5. 5-fold validation, StratifiedKFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=42), tuned
   parameters, early stopping (max 5000 iterations, patience 200):
     without new features : mean AUC 0.94192 (OOF 0.94191)
     with 4 new features  : mean AUC 0.94211 (OOF 0.94210)
   The features scored higher in 5 of 5 folds (mean gain +0.00019).
6. Final model: CatBoost retrained on 100% of train with a fixed number of
   iterations (3500 suggested; [CHECK] value actually used), then predicted the
   test set, which went through the same cleaning and feature steps as train.
   Submission contains probabilities of "Yes".
